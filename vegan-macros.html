Vegan Macros — FAQ

How much protein do vegans need?

Vegans need slightly more protein than omnivores because plant proteins are less bioavailable. Aim for 1.4–2.0 g per kg of body weight. Good vegan sources: lentils, tofu, tempeh, seitan, chickpeas, and hemp seeds.

Are carbs bad on a vegan diet?

No. Whole-food carbs (beans, lentils, oats, brown rice, fruit) are excellent — they provide fiber, vitamins, and sustained energy. Refined carbs (white bread, sugar) are the ones to limit.

How do I get enough fat as a vegan?

Easy: nuts, seeds, avocado, olives, coconut, and plant oils. These are the best fat sources. Omega-3s are important too — add flaxseeds, chia seeds, or walnuts daily.

Should I supplement on a vegan diet?

Yes. Vitamin B12 is essential (fortified foods or supplements). Vitamin D, iron, zinc, and omega-3s may also need attention. Check our Vitamin D Calculator for personalized sun/supplement guidance.

Can I build muscle on a vegan diet?

Absolutely. Plant protein works just as well for muscle growth when you eat enough of it. Focus on soy, legumes, and grains. Our Strength Training Protein Calculator can help you find the right amount.
