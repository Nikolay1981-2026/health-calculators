About This Project

Specialized health tools for people who don't fit the "standard adult" mold.

Why this site exists

Most health calculators assume a healthy 30-year-old with no special conditions. But what if you have type 2 diabetes, or you're 75, or you're pregnant, or you're a vegan athlete? Standard tools often give you the wrong numbers.

This site fixes that. We built 10 health calculators for specific groups — so the numbers are actually useful, not just generic averages.

What you'll find here

  • Diabetic Calorie Calculator — daily calories for people with type 2 diabetes, factoring in weight goals and blood sugar control.
  • Senior BMI Calculator — healthy BMI ranges for adults over 65 (they are different from standard adult ranges).
  • Athlete Water Calculator — hydration needs based on workout intensity, duration, sweat rate, and climate.
  • Vegan Macro Calculator — plant-based carb, fat, and protein targets (plant protein needs are slightly higher).
  • Hypertension Heart Rate Calculator — safe exercise zones for people with high blood pressure.
  • Pregnancy Calorie Calculator — how many extra calories you need during each trimester.
  • Strength Training Protein Calculator — optimal protein intake for muscle growth and fat loss.
  • Teen Sleep Calculator — best bedtime for teenagers based on their wake-up time and age.
  • Vitamin D Calculator — personalized vitamin D needs based on latitude, skin type, and sun exposure.
  • Low-Carb Carb Calculator — daily net carb targets for keto, moderate low-carb, or liberal low-carb diets.

How we calculate

We use established formulas from peer-reviewed research: the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories, WHO BMI ranges (adjusted for age), American Academy of Sleep Medicine guidelines, and recommendations from the American Diabetes Association, American Heart Association, and ACOG.

Results are estimates — not medical advice. Your real needs depend on your individual health, medications, and doctor's guidance.

No data collection

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored, or shared. No accounts. No tracking of your inputs.

Install as an app

This site is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your phone or computer — it will work offline and open like a regular app. Look for the "Install" prompt when you visit, or use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option.
