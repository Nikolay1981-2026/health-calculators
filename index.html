Frequently Asked Questions

Why specialized health calculators?

Generic calorie counters assume a "standard" adult. But a diabetic, a senior, an athlete, and a pregnant woman all have different needs. Our Diabetic Calorie Calculator, Senior BMI Calculator, and Athlete Water Calculator are built for specific groups so the numbers are actually useful.

Are these calculators medical advice?

No. These are educational tools that give estimates based on standard formulas. Always consult your doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions. If you have high blood pressure, check our Hypertension Heart Rate Calculator for safe exercise zones.

Where do the formulas come from?

We use established formulas from peer-reviewed research: the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories, WHO BMI ranges (adjusted for age), and guidelines from the American Diabetes Association. Our Vegan Macro Calculator uses plant-specific protein guidelines, and our Pregnancy Calorie Calculator follows ACOG recommendations.

How much protein do I need for strength training?

Most people need 1.6–2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight for muscle growth. Our Strength Training Protein Calculator gives you a personalized target based on your weight and training intensity.

How many carbs should I eat on keto or low-carb?

Keto usually means 20–50g of net carbs per day. Low-carb is typically 50–100g. Our Low-Carb Carb Calculator shows your specific target based on your goals.

How much sleep do teenagers actually need?

Teens need 8–10 hours of sleep per night — more than adults. But early school start times make this hard. Our Teen Sleep Calculator shows the best bedtime based on wake-up time.

How much vitamin D do I need if I live in a cloudy region?

People in northern latitudes (above 37°N) often need supplements in winter. Our Vitamin D Calculator estimates your needs based on latitude, skin type, and sun exposure.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, stored, or shared. No accounts required.

Can I install this as an app?

Yes. This site is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android or desktop Chrome, you'll see an "Install" prompt. On iPhone, tap "Share" → "Add to Home Screen". Works offline.

Are the calculators free?

Yes, 100% free. No sign-up, no email required, no hidden fees. We may show ads to keep the site running, but the tools are always free.
